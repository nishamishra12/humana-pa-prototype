WHY RECALL, NOT PRECISION
The group that matters: cases that need a person.
Recall
Of the cases that needed a person, how many we flagged. A miss is a wrong approval. That is the dangerous failure.
Precision
Of the cases we flagged, how many really needed a person. A miss is an extra flag. It costs a few minutes of nurse time.
So we push recall up, and we accept more extra flags.
