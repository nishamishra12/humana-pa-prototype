THE POLICY OWNER · PART 1
The policy owner builds the library.
Before go-live. The biggest piece of work.
Policies shown are public Medicare policies. The AI drafts. It never publishes. Every code is stored with its source.
POLICY OWNER
A person
PA DESK
ETL + AI, and plain code
PERSON
Picks the policy
A CMS policy, or the plan's own PDF
1
The owner starts by choosing a policy for a procedure we want to cover.
PERSON
Adds the billing codes
Each code, with its source
2
Along with the billing codes that tell us when this policy applies. Each code keeps its source.
ETL + AI
Reads the policy
Titles, paragraphs, tables and pages
3
The ETL step with AI inside reads the policy and keeps its structure.
AI
Drafts the key facts
A rule for each, with its source sentence
4
The AI turns each requirement into a rule, and names the fact a nurse must check.
CODE
Checks the draft
Every quote, number and fact
5
Plain code checks the draft. Anything that fails goes to the owner. It is never dropped.
PERSON
Reviews every rule
Approve, edit or reject
6
The owner reads each rule beside the official text. The AI drafts. A person signs.
PERSON
Links and publishes
Codes to a service. A versioned library
7
They link the codes to a service and publish. Every version is kept and can be rolled back.
The result: for every procedure we cover, the key facts to check when a packet arrives.
