DECISIONS AND TRADE-OFFS
Three decisions. A person at both ends.
Trust before speed
CMS, February 2024: a coverage decision must rest on the patient's own record, not on a prediction from large data sets.
BUSINESS DECISION · THE READER
Learn from past decisions, or read the policy?
THE CHOICE
Read this patient's packet against the policy. Do not train on Humana's past decisions.
WHY
Past decisions carry old mistakes and old bias. CMS: decide on the patient's own record.
THE COST
No shortcut from years of history. Every rule is built and approved.
HOW HISTORY HELPS
Test data for the evals. A ranking of which services to onboard next.
TRADE-OFF · THE RULEBOOK
Who approves the key facts?
THE CHOICE
The owner approves every key fact the AI drafts. The AI does not approve its own.
WHY NOT AUTO
Too many floods the nurse with pends. Too few lets a requirement through.
THE COST
Owner time. But it is one time for each policy.
HOW WE RELAX IT
Her decisions become a test set. High precision and recall on new policies: auto.
TRADE-OFF · THE PACKETS
How cautious is the AI?
THE CHOICE
Recall over precision. When unsure, send it to a person.
WHY
A miss is a wrong approval. A false alarm costs a few minutes.
THE COST
Some false alarms, so nurses see more flagged cases.
HOW WE RELAX IT
Watch the nurses. Many approvals on flags: raise precision. A miss: fix it first.
Start cautious. Watch what people do. Relax the AI only as fast as the evidence allows.
