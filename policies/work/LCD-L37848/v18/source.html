LCD L37848: Lumbar Spinal Fusion

<p><strong><span style="line-height: 115%;">Definitions and Scope</span></strong></p> <p><span style="line-height: 115%;">This local coverage determination (LCD) discusses fusions of the lumbar spine.</span></p> <p><strong><span style="line-height: 115%;">Background</span></strong></p> <p><span style="line-height: 115%;">This&nbsp;LCD will discuss indications for surgical fusion of the lumbar spine. Total disc arthroplasty is not covered by this LCD.</span></p> <p><strong style="mso-bidi-font-weight: normal;"><span style="line-height: 115%; font-family: 'Verdana','sans-serif'; font-size: 10.5pt;">Requirements</span></strong></p> <p><span style="font-family: Times New Roman; font-size: medium;"> <span style="line-height: 115%; font-family: 'Verdana','sans-serif'; font-size: 10.5pt;">Lumbar fusion must be performed by a qualified surgeon in an operating room with the necessary equipment. For elective surgeries, providers should verify that the necessary equipment and hardware to perform the procedure on a patient is available prior to the induction of anesthesia.</span></span></p> <p><span style="font-family: Times New Roman; font-size: medium;"> <span style="line-height: 115%; font-family: 'Verdana','sans-serif'; font-size: 10.5pt;">Documentation must demonstrate that the patient met at least&nbsp;1 of the indications for the procedure as described below. Where possible, there must be documented shared decision making with the patient or the individual who is serving as the proxy decision maker for the patient with the appropriate discussion of anticipated risks and benefits of the procedure.</span></span></p> <p><strong style="mso-bidi-font-weight: normal;"><span style="line-height: 115%; font-family: 'Verdana','sans-serif'; font-size: 10.5pt;">Indications for lumbar fusion include any of&nbsp;the following.</span></strong></p> <p><span style="line-height: 115%; font-family: 'Verdana','sans-serif'; font-size: 10.5pt;">A patient must have at least&nbsp;1 of the following:</span></p> <p style="padding-left: 40px;"><span style="line-height: 115%; font-family: 'Verdana','sans-serif'; font-size: 10.5pt;"><span style="line-height: 115%; font-family: 'Verdana','sans-serif'; font-size: 10.5pt;">1. Radiographic or clinical evidence of instability due to any&nbsp;1 of the following:</span></span></p> <p style="padding-left: 80px;">&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;<span style="line-height: 115%; font-family: 'Verdana','sans-serif'; font-size: 10.5pt;">a. Congenital deformities,</span></p> <p style="padding-left: 80px;">&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;<span style="line-height: 115%; font-family: 'Verdana','sans-serif'; font-size: 10.5pt;">b. Trauma,</span></p> <p style="padding-left: 80px;">&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;<span style="line-height: 115%; font-family: 'Verdana','sans-serif'; font-size: 10.5pt;">c. Fractures,</span></p> <p style="padding-left: 80px;">&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;<span style="line-height: 115%; font-family: 'Verdana','sans-serif'; font-size: 10.5pt;">d. Chronic degenerative conditions,</span></p> <p style="padding-left: 80px;">&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; <span style="line-height: 115%; font-family: 'Verdana','sans-serif'; font-size: 10.5pt;">e. Tumor,</span></p> <p style="padding-left: 80px;">&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; <span style="line-height: 115%; font-family: 'Verdana','sans-serif'; font-size: 10.5pt;">f. Infection,</span></p> <p style="padding-left: 80px;">&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; <span style="line-height: 115%; font-family: 'Verdana','sans-serif'; font-size: 10.5pt;">g. Erosive conditions,</span></p> <p style="padding-left: 80px;">&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; <span style="line-height: 115%; font-family: 'Verdana','sans-serif'; font-size: 10.5pt;">h. Space-occupying lesions, <span style="text-decoration: underline;"><strong>or</strong></span></span></p> <p style="padding-left: 80px;">&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; <span style="line-height: 115%; font-family: 'Verdana','sans-serif'; font-size: 10.5pt;">i. Iatrogenic causes, including expected instability as a consequence of another medically necessary spine procedure.</span></p> <p style="padding-left: 40px;"><span style="font-family: Times New Roman; font-size: medium;">&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; &nbsp;<span style="line-height: 115%; font-family: 'Verdana','sans-serif'; font-size: 10.5pt;">If more than&nbsp;1 level is fused under this indication, the record must reflect that each fused level is affected by&nbsp;1 of the above conditions.</span></span></p> <p style="padding-left: 40px;">&nbsp;<span style="line-height: 115%; font-family: 'Verdana','sans-serif'; font-size: 10.5pt;">2. Symptomatic spinal deformity (in the absence of instability or neural compression) which meets the following criteria:</span></p> <p style="padding-left: 40px;">&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; <span style="line-height: 115%; font-family: 'Verdana','sans-serif'; font-size: 10.5pt;">Both of the first&nbsp;2 criteria must be met:</span></p> <p style="padding-left: 80px;">&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; <span style="line-height: 115%; font-family: 'Verdana','sans-serif'; font-size: 10.5pt;">a. Functional limitation in daily activities due to back pain or discomfort <strong style="mso-bidi-font-weight: normal;"><u>and</u></strong></span></p> <p style="padding-left: 80px;">&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; <span style="line-height: 115%; font-family: 'Verdana','sans-serif'; font-size: 10.5pt;">b. Nonresponse to at least&nbsp;1 year of non-operative treatment</span></p> <p style="padding-left: 40px;">&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; <span style="line-height: 115%; font-family: 'Verdana','sans-serif'; font-size: 10.5pt;">Any&nbsp;1 of these&nbsp;3 criteria must be met:</span></p> <p style="padding-left: 80px;">&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; <span style="line-height: 115%; font-family: 'Verdana','sans-serif'; font-size: 10.5pt;">c. Sagittal or coronal imbalance by at least 5 cm as measured on radiographic imaging of the entire spine, <strong style="mso-bidi-font-weight: normal;"><u>or</u></strong></span></p> <p style="padding-left: 80px;">&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; <span style="line-height: 115%; font-family: 'Verdana','sans-serif'; font-size: 10.5pt;">d. Progression of deformity by at least 10 degrees, <strong style="mso-bidi-font-weight: normal;"><u>or</u></strong></span></p> <p style="padding-left: 80px;">&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; <span style="line-height: 115%; font-family: 'Verdana','sans-serif'; font-size: 10.5pt;">e. Scoliotic curvature of greater than 30 degrees</span></p> <p style="padding-left: 40px;">&nbsp;<span style="line-height: 115%; font-family: 'Verdana','sans-serif'; font-size: 10.5pt;">3. Revision surgery for pseudarthrosis following an initial spine surgery if the following conditions are all met.</span></p> <p style="padding-left: 80px;">&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; <span style="line-height: 115%; font-family: 'Verdana','sans-serif'; font-size: 10.5pt;">a. The patient had a period of reduced pain initially following surgery <strong style="mso-bidi-font-weight: normal;"><u>and</u></strong></span></p> <p style="padding-left: 80px;">&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; <span style="line-height: 115%; font-family: 'Verdana','sans-serif'; font-size: 10.5pt;">b. The time since the prior surgery has been at least&nbsp;1 year <strong style="mso-bidi-font-weight: normal;"><u>and </u></strong></span></p> <p style="padding-left: 80px;">&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; <span style="line-height: 115%; font-family: 'Verdana','sans-serif'; font-size: 10.5pt;">c. There is clear radiographic evidence of pseudarthrosis <span style="mso-spacerun: yes;">&nbsp;</span><strong style="mso-bidi-font-weight: normal;"><u>and</u></strong></span></p> <p style="padding-left: 80px;">&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; <span style="line-height: 115%; font-family: 'Verdana','sans-serif'; font-size: 10.5pt;">d. The patient has exhausted available conservative treatment measures</span></p> <p style="padding-left: 80px;"><span style="font-family: Times New Roman; font-size: medium;"> <span style="line-height: 115%; font-family: 'Verdana','sans-serif'; font-size: 10.5pt;">The medical record must clearly reflect which conservative treatments the patient has tried or is not a candidate for and why, including medical therapies, physical and exercise therapies and injections. Outcomes for fusion in revision surgery, usually do not lead to pain relief and as such fusion is considered a last resort treatment option only when all other treatment options have failed. This information must be communicated to the patient prior to surgery to allow for appropriate shared decision making with a well-informed patient. The medical record must reflect that this counseling was done and that the patient wished to undergo surgery with the appropriately informed consent.</span></span></p> <p style="padding-left: 40px;">&nbsp;<span style="line-height: 115%; font-family: 'Verdana','sans-serif'; font-size: 10.5pt;">4. Symptomatic compression of neural elements for which disc excision is necessary for decompression. The record must reflect disc compression of neural elements for each level fused based on this indication.</span></p><p><span style="line-height: 115%; font-family: 'Verdana','sans-serif'; font-size: 10.5pt;">Title XVIII of the Social Security Act, &sect;1862(a)(1)(A) allows coverage and payment for only those services that are considered to be reasonable and necessary for the diagnosis or treatment of illness or injury or to improve the functioning of a malformed body member</span></p> <p><span style="line-height: 115%; font-family: 'Verdana','sans-serif'; font-size: 10.5pt;"><span style="line-height: 115%; font-family: 'Verdana','sans-serif'; font-size: 10.5pt;">Title XVIII of the Social Security Act, &sect;1862(a)(1)(D) in the case of clinical care items and services provided with respect to research and experimentation</span></span></p> <p><span style="line-height: 115%; font-family: 'Verdana','sans-serif'; font-size: 10.5pt;"><span style="line-height: 115%; font-family: 'Verdana','sans-serif'; font-size: 10.5pt;"><span style="line-height: 115%; font-family: 'Verdana','sans-serif'; font-size: 10.5pt;">CMS Internet-Only Manual, Pub. 100-08, Medicare Program Integrity Manual, Chapter 13, &sect;13.5.1 General Requirements (states that "reasonable and necessary" services must be ordered and/or furnished by qualified personnel)</span></span></span></p>
