# Spinal cord stimulator

- **Service to create:** Spinal cord stimulator
- **Describe it as:** Implanting spinal cord stimulator leads and the pulse generator for chronic pain.
- **Billing code to start with:** 63685 (also 63650)
- **Policy to build:** LCD L36204 (Noridian)

## The two packets

### A_straightforward_mitchell_permanent_implant.pdf  (Straightforward)

**What it contains:** Failed back surgery syndrome. Medications, physical therapy and injections tried. A psychological screen, a patient education record and a 10-day trial with 63% pain reduction, 50% less medication and better walking.

**What to expect:** Approve. The trial result clears the 50% bar and the screening is documented.

### B_complex_lopez_weak_trial.pdf  (Complex)

**What it contains:** The trial gave about 33% pain reduction with no drop in opioids and no measured function. A urine screen positive for a drug that was not prescribed. The psychological evaluation is not done. Only home exercise, no formal physical therapy.

**What to expect:** Escalate or pend. The trial misses the 50% threshold and the screening items are missing.

The expected result is a guide. It depends on which rules you approved when you reviewed the policy.

All patient data is made up.
