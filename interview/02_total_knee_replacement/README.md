# Total knee replacement

- **Service to create:** Total knee replacement
- **Describe it as:** Total knee replacement for arthritis of the knee, first-time and revision.
- **Billing code to start with:** 27447
- **Policy to build:** LCD L36575 (Noridian)

## The two packets

### A_straightforward_evans_right_knee.pdf  (Straightforward)

**What it contains:** Weight-bearing X-ray with grade 4 osteoarthritis, severe pain and limited walking, six months of NSAIDs, two injections, 12 physical therapy visits and a cane.

**What to expect:** Approve. Imaging, functional limits and failed conservative treatment are all documented.

### B_complex_jennings_laterality_mismatch.pdf  (Complex)

**What it contains:** The request says left knee, the exam and plan are about the right knee, and the outside MRI does not say which side. No weight-bearing X-ray report. Conservative treatment is 3 weeks of ibuprofen and a refused physical therapy course. Uncontrolled diabetes (A1c 9.3%) and warfarin.

**What to expect:** Pend. Ask which knee, ask for the X-ray report, and ask what conservative treatment was tried.
