# TMS for depression

- **Service to create:** TMS for depression
- **Describe it as:** Magnetic stimulation treatment for depression that has not responded to other treatment.
- **Billing code to start with:** 90867 (also 90868, 90869)
- **Policy to build:** LCD L34641 (WPS)

## The two packets

### A_straightforward_foster.pdf  (Straightforward)

**What it contains:** Severe recurrent depression, PHQ-9 22. Two adequate medication trials from different classes plus a third, 16 sessions of CBT with no improvement on PHQ-9, ordered by a psychiatrist who examined the patient, and a clean safety screen.

**What to expect:** Approve. Severity, failed treatments, therapy and prescriber all meet the policy.

### B_complex_brennan.pdf  (Complex)

**What it contains:** Moderate depression, PHQ-9 15. Only one medication class tried, for a few weeks each. Psychotherapy not started. A seizure at age 19 during alcohol withdrawal. Ordered by a nurse practitioner, not a psychiatrist who examined the patient.

**What to expect:** Pend or escalate. Several policy requirements are missing and a seizure history is a safety limit.

The expected result is a guide. It depends on which rules you approved when you reviewed the policy.

All patient data is made up.
