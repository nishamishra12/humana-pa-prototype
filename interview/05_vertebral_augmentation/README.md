# Vertebral augmentation

- **Service to create:** Vertebral augmentation
- **Describe it as:** Cement into a painful vertebral compression fracture: vertebroplasty and kyphoplasty.
- **Billing code to start with:** 22514 (also 22513)
- **Policy to build:** LCD L38213 (WPS)

## The two packets

### A_straightforward_collins_acute_fracture.pdf  (Straightforward)

**What it contains:** An acute L1 osteoporotic fracture, 5 weeks old, with an MRI from 14 days ago showing bone marrow edema. Pain of 7 to 9, worsening despite 4 weeks of non-surgical care, 38% height loss, no neurologic deficit, no infection and normal coagulation.

**What to expect:** Approve. Timing, imaging, pain and conservative care all meet the criteria.

### B_complex_stevens_old_fracture_cancer_history.pdf  (Complex)

**What it contains:** A fracture about 4 months old, past the acute and subacute window. Only an X-ray from 3 months ago, no MRI. Pain of 6, 3 weeks of opioids and no brace or physical therapy. A prostate cancer history with a rising PSA and bone scan uptake, so the fracture may not be osteoporotic. Warfarin with an INR of 2.6.

**What to expect:** Escalate. The fracture may be metastatic or too old, and imaging is missing.

The expected result is a guide. It depends on which rules you approved when you reviewed the policy.
