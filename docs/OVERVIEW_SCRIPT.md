# Overview slide: speaker script

In my own voice. Plain, active sentences. For the simple two-part overview slide.

## Script
The solution has two parts.

Part 1 builds the library. Think of it as onboarding a new service. It happens once for each service, before go-live, and it is the biggest piece of work. The policy owner puts in a service, its billing codes and its policies. What comes out is a list of key facts, each with a rule.

Let me explain a key fact in simple words. A key fact is something the policy says must be in the packet for the request to be covered. Say the policy needs a hospital stay of at least two midnights. The key fact is length of stay. The rule is at least two midnights. I'll go deeper on this in the next slide.

Part 2 is the daily work. A packet comes in from a provider. The AI checks it against those key facts and gives the nurse a recommendation, with the evidence for each one. The nurse confirms. The medical director decides the hard cases, and only they can deny.

A person is in the loop in both parts. And Part 2 only uses what Part 1 approved.
